About

Siren Audio Mixer lets you set the volume and a sound preset for each tab separately. Run two calls, or a call and music, without them drowning each other out.

Click the icon on a tab that's playing audio to control it. Presets make voices sound different (Clear, Background, Muffled, Radio), so two at once are easier to follow.

The list shows the tabs playing audio right now. Volume and preset stay with the tab, so anything it plays later keeps the same settings until the tab closes.

While a tab is controlled its lock is closed, and Chrome keeps that tab's fullscreen inside the tab. Click the lock to release the tab: audio goes back to it and fullscreen behaves normally. Your volume and preset are kept, so clicking the open lock takes the tab back with the same settings. F11 window fullscreen always works.

Privacy policy

  • Tab audio is processed on your device only. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • No personal data, browsing history or analytics are collected.
  • No network requests are made, and no servers or third parties are involved.
  • Volume and preset settings are kept in memory only and are cleared when the tab or Chrome closes.
  • Permissions: tabCapture and activeTab to read the audio of the tab you choose, offscreen to keep playing it after the popup closes, and tabs to list which tabs are playing audio, with their title and icon.
